    How ED Medication Works in Auburn

    Most ED treatment uses PDE5 inhibitors, a drug class that blocks the enzyme which shuts down blood flow into the penis after arousal, so the erection is easier to get and keep. They do not cause an erection on their own. Arousal is still required. Sildenafil is the active ingredient in Viagra, and tadalafil is the one in Cialis, and both are sold as FDA-approved generics.

    Who Men's Sexual Health Care Is For

    This care is for adult men who have trouble getting or keeping an erection firm enough for sex, or whose interest in sex has dropped in a way that bothers them. Risk rises with age and with diabetes or high blood pressure. Stress counts too. Alcohol and short sleep are common contributors, and some blood pressure or antidepressant medications can cause it.

    Sildenafil for As-Needed Use in Auburn

    Sildenafil is usually taken 30 to 60 minutes before sex, comes in 25, 50 and 100 mg tablets, and works for about 4 to 6 hours after a dose. Taking it after a big greasy dinner slows it down. Empty stomach works best. Most men start at 50 mg, adjusted up or down from there, with no more than 1 dose in 24 hours.

    Tadalafil, As Needed or Daily

    Tadalafil taken as needed, usually 10 or 20 mg, can work for up to 36 hours, which is why some people call it the weekend pill and prefer it for less planning. A low daily dose of 2.5 or 5 mg removes the need to plan. Food barely affects it. Daily tadalafil is also FDA-approved for urinary symptoms of an enlarged prostate.

    Chewables, Dissolving Tablets and Troches in Auburn

    Daily ED chewables, rapid dissolve tablets and troches are compounded forms of sildenafil or tadalafil that melt in the mouth or under the tongue instead of being swallowed with water. They help men who dislike pills or want a faster-feeling option. These are not FDA-approved. Each compounded formula has its own strength, set by your provider and listed on the label.

    PT-141 for Low Desire

    PT-141, or bremelanotide, works on melanocortin receptors in the brain and is aimed at desire rather than blood flow, which makes it a different tool from sildenafil or tadalafil. For men, it is prescribed off-label. Nausea is common. It also raises blood pressure briefly, so heart screening matters even more, and some men use it alongside a PDE5 inhibitor.

    Side Effects and Safety in Auburn

    Common side effects of ED pills include headache, facial flushing, stuffy nose and indigestion, and they usually fade within a few hours as the medication wears off. Tadalafil can cause back or muscle aches. Sildenafil can tint vision blue. Mixing PDE5 inhibitors with nitroglycerin or amyl nitrite poppers can drop blood pressure dangerously, and an erection over 4 hours is an emergency.

Nitrates and riociguat rule out PDE5 inhibitors completely. Alpha blockers need care. A home or pharmacy blood pressure reading from the last 6 months helps.
