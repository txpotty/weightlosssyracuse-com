    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Syracuse, NY

    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Syracuse

    Most modern weight loss shots are GLP-1 medicines. They copy a hormone your gut releases after eating, which slows stomach emptying, signals fullness to the brain and helps regulate blood sugar after a meal. Semaglutide acts on GLP-1 alone. Tirzepatide acts on both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, which may explain its larger average effect in trials.

    Who Weight Loss Injections Are For

    FDA-approved injections are for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. Adults with a BMI of 27 or more also qualify if they have a weight-related condition such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol or sleep apnea. They work alongside diet and exercise. They are not for pregnancy or certain thyroid cancer histories.

    Injection Dosing Schedule in Syracuse

    Every weekly injection starts low. Wegovy begins at 0.25 mg and climbs every four weeks through 0.5, 1 and 1.7 mg to a maintenance dose of 2.4 mg, though some people stay at 1.7 mg. Zepbound begins at 2.5 mg and can go up by 2.5 mg at a time to a top of 15 mg. Same day each week.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Appetite often drops in the first week. Weight change during the first month is usually modest because the starting dose is low, and nausea is most common in the day or two after each injection. Large trials reported average losses near 15 percent with semaglutide 2.4 mg over 68 weeks and near 20 percent with tirzepatide 15 mg over 72 weeks. Results vary.

    Injection Side Effects in Syracuse

    Nausea is the classic one. Diarrhea, constipation, vomiting, heartburn and mild redness at the injection site are also common, especially in the days after starting or raising the dose. Eating slowly and skipping greasy food on shot day takes the edge off. Rarer serious risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems and dehydration-related kidney strain.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening starts with BMI and medical history. Next comes your medication list, with attention to insulin, glipizide-type drugs and oral contraceptives, which tirzepatide can make less effective around dose increases. A family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 is a hard stop. Pregnancy and breastfeeding are too.

    Brand-Name vs Compounded Injections in Syracuse

    Wegovy and Zepbound are FDA-approved for weight management. Ozempic and Mounjaro share those ingredients but carry type 2 diabetes labels instead. Compounded versions of semaglutide and tirzepatide come from licensed compounding pharmacies and lack FDA approval, which means no FDA review of their safety, effectiveness or manufacturing. Some patients choose them for availability.
