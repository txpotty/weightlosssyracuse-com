    How Zepbound Treatment Works in Syracuse, NY

    How Zepbound Works in Syracuse

    Tirzepatide acts on two gut hormone receptors. By activating both the GIP and GLP-1 receptors, it slows stomach emptying, signals fullness to the brain and helps the body manage blood sugar after eating. Many notice fewer cravings and smaller portions. It is injected under the skin of the stomach, thigh or upper arm once a week.

    Who Zepbound Is For

    Zepbound is approved for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. Adults with a BMI of 27 or more also qualify if they have a condition tied to weight, such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol or sleep apnea. A separate approval covers moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity. It is not approved for children.

    Zepbound Dosing Schedule in Syracuse

    Everyone starts at 2.5 mg. That first month is about letting your stomach adjust, not about weight loss, so modest early changes are normal. After four weeks the dose usually moves up in 2.5 mg steps to 5, 7.5, 10, 12.5 and up to 15 mg, with at least four weeks at each. Maintenance doses are typically 5, 10 or 15 mg.

    What to Expect Week by Week in Syracuse

    The first month is mostly quiet. Appetite often drops within days, while nausea or an upset stomach shows up most in the day or two after each injection and after every dose increase. In the SURMOUNT-1 trial, adults on the highest dose lost about 20 percent of body weight on average over 72 weeks. Individual results vary.

    Zepbound Side Effects

    Nausea and diarrhea come up most. Other common effects include constipation, heartburn, belly pain and mild injection site reactions, and they usually fade as your body adjusts. Rarer and more serious problems include pancreatitis, gallstones, dehydration that harms the kidneys and allergic reactions. Zepbound carries a boxed warning about thyroid C-cell tumors seen in rodents.

    Eligibility and Screening in Syracuse

    Your provider starts with height, weight and BMI. Next comes your medical history and every medication you take, with special attention to insulin, sulfonylureas and birth control pills. People with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 cannot take Zepbound, and it is not used during pregnancy. Labs may be requested.

    Zepbound Compared With Related Options in Syracuse

    Zepbound and Mounjaro are the same medicine. Zepbound carries the weight management label and Mounjaro carries the type 2 diabetes label, which matters for insurance and for how your provider writes the prescription. In the SURMOUNT-5 head-to-head trial, tirzepatide produced more average weight loss than Wegovy. Compounded tirzepatide comes from compounding pharmacies and is not FDA-approved.
