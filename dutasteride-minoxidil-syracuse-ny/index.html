    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Syracuse, NY

    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Syracuse

    Dutasteride blocks both type 1 and type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzymes that turn testosterone into DHT. Because it hits both, it lowers DHT in the blood by more than 90 percent, compared with roughly 70 percent for finasteride, which blocks only type 2. Less DHT, less shrinking. Minoxidil adds a separate benefit by lengthening the growth phase. The pair covers cause and growth.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss who did not get enough from finasteride. Most providers want to see about a year of consistent finasteride use with progress photos before switching, since some men simply need more time on the first drug. Fast early loss can move the timeline up. It is not used in women who could become pregnant.

    Dosing and Schedule in Syracuse

    Dutasteride is FDA-approved at 0.5 mg daily for an enlarged prostate and is used off-label at that dose for hair loss. Its long half-life lets some providers use fewer doses per week. Minoxidil goes on topically once or twice a day. Low-dose oral minoxidil is an off-label alternative.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Shedding can pick up in months one and two, usually from minoxidil. The early shed is normal and usually fades. Around month six, density often improves. Comparison trials favored dutasteride over finasteride for hair counts. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Dutasteride + Minoxidil in Syracuse

    Dutasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in a small share of men, similar to finasteride. Breast tenderness or enlargement is rare, and because the drug lingers for months, side effects may take longer to fade after stopping than they would with finasteride. Mood changes should be reported right away. Minoxidil can irritate the scalp. Oral minoxidil may cause body hair or swelling.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, mood history, sexual health and current medications. PSA numbers drop by roughly half, which your other doctors should factor in. No blood donation during use or for six months after. Women who might be pregnant should not handle them.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Syracuse

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the usual first step, and both parts have FDA approval for hair loss. The dutasteride pairing is more potent, off-label and longer-lasting in the body. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is a topical-only choice. Transplants help some men.
