    How Sermorelin Therapy Works in Syracuse, NY

    How Sermorelin Works in Syracuse

    Sermorelin is a 29 amino acid fragment of growth hormone releasing hormone. It binds the same pituitary receptors as your natural hormone and prompts growth hormone release in pulses, the way your body already does it at night. Growth hormone then raises IGF-1. The body's own feedback system still applies, so growth hormone rarely overshoots the way it can with synthetic hormone injections. The original brand, Geref, was discontinued in 2008, and today's sermorelin is compounded.

    Who Sermorelin Is Usually For

    Typical candidates are adults over 35 who notice poorer sleep, slower recovery or a softer middle despite steady habits. A low-normal IGF-1 for your age can make sermorelin easier to justify and easier to monitor. It is not for weight loss alone. People with active cancer, those who are pregnant or breastfeeding, and anyone with a pituitary tumor are not candidates.

    Sermorelin Dosing and Schedule in Syracuse

    Bedtime is the usual slot. Many compounded protocols start around 200 to 300 micrograms under the skin nightly, five to seven nights a week, though your provider sets your exact amount. Injecting on an empty stomach, about two hours after your last meal, may help. Most shipments are a freeze-dried powder that you reconstitute with bacteriostatic water and then refrigerate.

    What the First Three Months Look Like

    Weeks one and two are mostly about the routine. Deeper sleep is the earliest change some patients report, though many feel no difference yet. Between the first and third month, some adults describe better recovery after exercise, while changes in fat and muscle tend to lag behind and depend on diet. Your provider often rechecks IGF-1 around week six to eight.

    Sermorelin Side Effects in Syracuse

    Redness, itching or a small bump where you inject is the most common complaint. Flushing, headache, dizziness and mild nausea also show up, mostly in the first few weeks. A smaller group notices restlessness, poor sleep or an odd taste, while hives, throat swelling or wheezing mean you should seek emergency care right away. Report it, and the dose can come down.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Before any prescription, your provider reads the intake for cancer history, pituitary problems, thyroid disease, diabetes, sleep apnea and pregnancy plans. Expect a request for IGF-1, TSH and a basic metabolic panel before the first prescription. Low thyroid that has not been treated, or regular steroid use like prednisone, can weaken the effect, which is why the medication list matters. Lab results can be uploaded.

    Sermorelin Compared to Other Peptides in Syracuse

    People ask about CJC-1295 most. The CJC-1295 and ipamorelin combination works on the same axis with a longer action and a second receptor target. Tesamorelin is a stabilized cousin of sermorelin that is FDA-approved as Egrifta SV for abdominal fat in adults with HIV lipodystrophy. Prescription growth hormone replaces the hormone directly and is reserved for diagnosed deficiency.
