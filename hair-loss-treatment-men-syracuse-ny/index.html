    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Syracuse, NY

    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Syracuse

    Male pattern hair loss runs on DHT. DHT is a hormone made from testosterone, and in men who inherited a sensitivity to it, DHT slowly shrinks follicles at the temples and crown until they make thin, short hairs or none at all. Cutting DHT with finasteride protects follicles from further shrinkage. Minoxidil works on a separate track, lengthening the growth phase.

    Who Men's Treatment Is For

    It suits adult men with a receding hairline, thinning crown or diffuse thinning on top. It works best when there is still visible hair in the thinning areas, because follicles that have been bald for years rarely respond to any medicine. Earlier is better. Patchy bald spots, scalp pain or rapid loss call for a dermatologist visit. The provider checks your photos for the typical pattern.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Syracuse

    Finasteride is one 1 mg tablet a day, with or without food. Minoxidil comes as a topical used once or twice a day, or as a low-dose off-label tablet. A compounded combination spray is applied once a day. Dutasteride is a capsule taken daily.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Months one and two can bring extra shedding, especially with minoxidil. That shedding usually means older hairs are being pushed out by new growth, and it tends to settle by month three without any change in the plan. Around months three to six, most men see less loss and some thickening. Expect the full effect near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Men's Treatment in Syracuse

    Finasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in roughly one to two percent of men in trials. Most sexual side effects go away after stopping, and some fade on their own. Mood changes are rare but should be reported promptly. Topical minoxidil can dry or irritate the scalp. Oral minoxidil can cause extra body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure and every medication you take. Expect questions about mood history, libido and family planning, because each can change the plan. PSA readings drop on finasteride. Mention it at your next prostate screening.

    How Men's Options Compare in Syracuse

    Finasteride plus minoxidil is the standard starting pair for men. Dutasteride with minoxidil is a stronger DHT-blocking option, used off-label, for men who did not get enough from finasteride after about a year. Minoxidil with GHK-Cu is a topical-only blend for men who want to skip DHT blockers. Surgery is an option for some.
