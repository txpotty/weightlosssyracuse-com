    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Syracuse, NY

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in Syracuse

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. With less DHT around, the follicles on the crown and temples stop shrinking as quickly, and many hold their size for years instead of fading out. Minoxidil works on a separate track. It keeps hairs in their growing phase longer, so they come in thicker.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. It works best when started early, while follicles are still producing hair, because areas that have been smooth for years rarely come back with any medicine. Some women past menopause use it off-label. Women who could become pregnant cannot.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Syracuse

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Topical minoxidil is applied to a dry scalp once or twice daily, while low-dose oral minoxidil, often 1.25 to 2.5 mg, is an off-label daily pill some men prefer to avoid the mess. One spray daily also works. The combination spray is applied to the scalp each day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. That early shed usually comes from minoxidil pushing resting hairs out so new growing hairs can take their place, and it typically settles by month three. Around month six, density starts to improve. In the original finasteride trials, most men kept their hair over five years, and many saw regrowth. Full results show near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in Syracuse

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. Most of these fade after stopping, and mood changes need prompt reporting. Scalp dryness is the usual topical issue. Oral minoxidil can cause body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat. Topical finasteride may lower whole-body exposure.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. They also review every medication and supplement, and they ask whether you plan to have children soon, since finasteride can slightly lower sperm count in some men. PSA results drop by about half. Women who could become pregnant should not handle crushed or broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Syracuse

    Finasteride + minoxidil is the standard first combination for men. When finasteride falls short, dutasteride is the usual next step, off-label for hair. For men avoiding DHT blockers, there is a minoxidil and GHK-Cu blend. Minoxidil by itself leaves the hormonal cause untreated.
